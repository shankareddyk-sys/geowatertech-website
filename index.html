Our Complete Water Solutions

We handle every phase of water development from underground exploration to water coming out of your tap.

Hydrogeological Surveys

Scientific geophysical surveys to pinpoint aquifer locations, water depth, and optimal drilling points before mobilization.

Borehole Drilling

High-specification rig drilling for shallow and deep water wells with heavy-duty PVC or steel casing installation.

Pump & Solar Installations

Supply and fitting of high-efficiency AC/DC submersible pumps and solar power systems designed for continuous, off-grid water pumping.

Water Storage & Towers

Construction of elevated steel/plastic tank stands and water storage tank installation providing gravity-fed pressurized water supply.

Borehole Rehabilitation

Restoration, flushing, yield testing, and repair of low-yield or collapsed existing boreholes to bring them back to full productivity.

Water Quality Testing

Comprehensive physical, chemical, and biological lab analysis of borehole water to ensure safe drinking and irrigation standards.
